# Pathfinder EU

**Plan a medicine's route to the EU market — before you file.**

Pathfinder EU is a regulatory strategy engine for EU/EEA human medicines. Describe the product and it determines:

- **Procedure:** centralised (mandatory or optional), decentralised, mutual recognition or national, with the legal reason.
- **Legal basis and dossier:** Articles 8(3), 10(1), 10(3), 10(4), 10a, 10b, 10c, 14 and 16a of Directive 2001/83/EC.
- **Designations and expedited tools:** orphan designation criteria, PRIME, accelerated assessment, conditional MA, exceptional circumstances, scientific advice.
- **Paediatric obligations:** PIP, waiver or deferral (Regulation (EC) No 1901/2006).
- **EU HTA:** joint clinical assessment scope by date (Regulation (EU) 2021/2282).
- **Approval timeline:** dated milestones per procedure.
- **Protection & market entry:** data protection, market protection, orphan exclusivity, patent, SPC and paediatric extension, under current law or the 2026 reform (indicative).
- **Exportable strategy report** (PDF / Markdown).

Single static file, no backend, no cookies, no tracking.
